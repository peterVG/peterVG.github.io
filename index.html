About

I am an archivist. I have been working with technology for archival collections since earning my Master's degree in Archival Science in 1997. I am a Distinguished Alumnus of the University of British Columbia's iSchool.

I am a decentralizationist. I have been working with technology for decentralized systems since 2016 when I wrote my vision piece: Decentralized Autonomous Collections. This includes projects using ATProto, Arweave, IPFS, Cardano, and Polygon.

I am a AI researcher. I am working on the implementation of provenance features as well as the secure and privacy-protecting application of AI technologies to labour-intensive records management and research data management tasks such as retention scheduling, compliance reporting, archival appraisal, PII scans, etc.

My area of expertise is AI-driven software development for archival and research data systems as well as applying records management standards and archival science research to requirements for decentralized software architectures.

I am the creator of the open-source Archivematica and AtoM (AccessToMemory) software applications; together the most widely-deployed digital archives system across the globe.

I have worked with public sector institutions worldwide (including United Nations, World Bank, NATO) to provide industry-standard record-keeping technology and digital preservation services that range from writing strategy reports, analyzing system requirements, designing system architectures, developing software, and managing open-source projects. I have been a frequent conference speaker on these topics.

I am the founder of Artefactual Systems, Landano International, Orcfax, Arkrim, and Iona Systems.

Iona is a Canadian based company that incubates R&D projects focussed on the technology intersection of digital archives systems, blockchain and AI. Iona Systems also makes my consulting services available to clients working in this same domain.

Iona Systems launched the Orcfax and Landano companies and is currently developing the Arkrim.tech system, a next-generation digital archives and records management platform. Iona is also conducting research into the secure and privacy-protecting application of AI technologies to labour-intensive records management tasks including retention scheduling, archival appraisal as well as PII and compliance scans.
